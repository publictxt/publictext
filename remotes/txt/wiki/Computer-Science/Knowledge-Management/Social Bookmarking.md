Social bookmarking is an online practice where users save, organise, annotate, and share web page links on centralise public or community-driven platforms rather than storing them in a local browser.

A key feature is **Collaborative Tagging**, where users assign descriptive keywords (tags) to create shared [Folksonomies](Folksonomies.md) instead of strict directories
