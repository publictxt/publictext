[Pinboard](https://pinboard.in) is an ad-free, and paid social bookmarking website created by Maciej Cegłowski that launched in July 2009. 

**Pinboard.in** bought [Del.icio.us](wiki/Computer-Science/Software/Social%20Software/Del.icio.us.md) after Yahoo drove it into the ground.

On launch, it offered a lifelong membership for a fixed fee, and I took that offer
