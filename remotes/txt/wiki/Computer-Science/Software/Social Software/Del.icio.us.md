#social-bookmarking #social-software 

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delicious_(website)
- Delicious used a non-hierarchical classification system in which users could [tag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tag_\(metadata\) "Tag (metadata)") each of their bookmarks with freely chosen [index terms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_term) (generating a kind of [folksonomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folksonomy)). A combined view of everyone's bookmarks with a given tag was available; for instance, the URL `http://delicious.com/tag/wiki` displayed all of the most recent links tagged [Wiki](../../Knowledge-Management/Wiki.md). Its collective nature made it possible to view bookmarks added by other users
