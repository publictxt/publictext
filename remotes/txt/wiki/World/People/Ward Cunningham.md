- Ward Cunningham is an American computer programmer who invented the first wiki software and helped create modern agile software development methods
- Invented the [Wiki](../../Computer-Science/Knowledge-Management/Wiki.md) Cunningham developed the first wiki, called [WikiWikiWeb](../../Computer-Science/C2%20Wiki.md), in 1994 and launched it online in 1995. 
- Currently still working on the [FedWiki](../../Computer-Science/Knowledge-Management/FedWiki.md) Project - a decentralized, open-source collaborative knowledge tool that lets individual users host their own separate sites and "fork" pages from one another


## Software Development #dev
- **Agile Manifesto:** He was one of the co-authors of the Manifesto for Agile Software Development in 2001, which changed how teams build software. 
- **Design Patterns:** He helped pioneer software design patterns, which are reusable solutions to common programming problems.
- **Extreme Programming:** He helped create Extreme Programming, a method focused on improving software quality and responsiveness to changing customer requirements.

## Cunningham's Law

- **The Rule:** He is credited with [Cunningham's Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cunningham%27s_Law), which states that "the best way to get the right answer on the internet is not to ask a question, but to post the wrong answer".
- **The Meaning:** People on the internet are more eager to correct a mistake than they are to answer an open question
