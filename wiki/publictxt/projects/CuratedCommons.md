# CuratedCommons

Specification stage. Social bookmarking and curation on top of PublicTxt repositories.

It borrows its spirit from Creative Commons: open standards, portable content, community ownership. Commentary and curation live in plain-text repositories — public, forkable, subscribable — instead of inside a centralised platform.
